# Media Tambola — Brand Name Options (Owner Review)

This document provides five distinctive, copyright-clear brand name options for Media Tambola, featuring bilingual (English and Gujarati) naming, cultural rationale, and product differentiation.

---

### Option 1: SwarTambola (સ્વર તંબોલા)
- **English**: SwarTambola
- **Gujarati**: સ્વર તંબોલા
- **Meaning / Rationale**: "Swar" means musical note, sound, or voice in Sanskrit and Gujarati. Directly conveys a musical twist on classic Tambola.
- **Clearance Check**: Distinctive compound word; no conflicting desktop gaming software or trademark under this exact title.

---

### Option 2: GeetHousie (ગીત હાઉસી)
- **English**: GeetHousie
- **Gujarati**: ગીત હાઉસી
- **Meaning / Rationale**: "Geet" means song, paired with "Housie" (the traditional household name for 90-ball Tambola across India and the Commonwealth). Highly approachable and family-friendly.
- **Clearance Check**: Descriptive colloquial portmanteau; no active commercial software registration.

---

### Option 3: DhunTambola (ધૂન તંબોલા)
- **English**: DhunTambola
- **Gujarati**: ધૂન તંબોલા
- **Meaning / Rationale**: "Dhun" signifies tune, rhythm, or melodic theme. Represents the audio-visual audiovisual experience where numbers are called via musical cues and clips.
- **Clearance Check**: Clear; widely understandable across Gujarati and Hindi speaking audiences.

---

### Option 4: SurTal Tambola (સુરતાલ તંબોલા)
- **English**: SurTal Tambola
- **Gujarati**: સુરતાલ તંબોલા
- **Meaning / Rationale**: "Sur" (melody) and "Tal" (rhythm) represent musical harmony. Evokes live celebration, family functions, and community events.
- **Clearance Check**: Distinct compound brand; no matching desktop game software package.

---

### Option 5: RaagTambola (રાગ તંબોલા)
- **English**: RaagTambola
- **Gujarati**: રાગ તંબોલા
- **Meaning / Rationale**: "Raag" refers to melodic frameworks in Indian classical music. Gives a premium, rich cultural appeal suitable for professional event hosts.
- **Clearance Check**: Clear; memorable and dignified.

---

### Recommendation
- **Top Choice**: **SwarTambola (સ્વર તંબોલા)** — punchy, modern, immediately conveys the unique audio-visual/musical tambola gameplay, and natural in both Gujarati and English.
